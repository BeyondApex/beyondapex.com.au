Support

Contact

Email welcome@beyondapex.com.au

Response times vary, but Beyond Apex will respond as soon as practical. This support channel is not monitored for emergencies.

When reporting a problem, include the app version and build, iPhone model and iOS version, the feature and approximate time of the issue, and any relevant route or stop identifier. You may attach screenshots after removing personal information. Do not send exact home or work locations, API credentials, service tokens or other sensitive information.

Common fixes

Live vehicles or arrivals are unavailable

Check your internet connection, reopen the app and try again. Transit feeds and the Metro Apex service can be delayed or temporarily unavailable. Confirm urgent travel information with official Adelaide Metro channels.

Nearby stops or navigation cannot find your location

Open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Metro Apex and allow location access. Approximate location can support map, nearby-stop and trip-planning features. Precise Location is required for active navigation. Location can remain disabled, but location-based features will be limited.

Reminders do not appear

Open iOS Settings → Notifications → Metro Apex and allow notifications. iOS controls background scheduling, so reminders are not guaranteed to run at an exact time.

A Live Activity remains after a trip

End the journey in Metro Apex. If needed, dismiss the Live Activity from the Lock Screen and reopen the app.

Delete saved data

Delete favourites, Saved Places, recent planner destinations, reminders and scheduled trips with their in-app controls. Deleting Metro Apex removes its normal on-device app data. Metro Apex has no user account to delete.
