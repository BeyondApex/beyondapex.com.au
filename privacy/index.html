Metro Apex AUS

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 September 2026

Metro Apex AUS is an independent Adelaide public-transport app provided by Beyond Apex (ABN 42 983 884 502). It is not affiliated with Adelaide Metro or the Department for Infrastructure and Transport, South Australia.

Summary

  • No advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Favourites, Saved Places, recent destinations, reminders, scheduled trips, preferences and notes are stored on your device. Metro Apex has no user accounts.
  • Location is optional. Approximate location can support map position, nearby stops and trip planning. Active navigation requests precise location.
  • Our service and infrastructure providers process limited network and security information, including IP address and request metadata.

Information stored on your device

The app may store favourites, Saved Places, recent planner destinations, recently viewed stops, reminders, scheduled trips, local-notification state, appearance and map preferences, and a short-lived service token held in the iOS Keychain. You can remove saved items with the app’s controls. Deleting the app removes its normal app-container data; iOS may retain Keychain items under Apple’s platform behaviour, but an expired token does not identify a Metro Apex account.

When you choose to share a completed journey, the app creates a temporary PDF. It attempts to remove the file after sharing and removes receipt files older than 24 hours when another receipt is created. A recipient may retain a shared copy under their own privacy terms.

Location information

With your permission, Metro Apex uses approximate or precise location to show your map position, find nearby stops and plan trips from your position. Active navigation requests precise location to track journey progress and stop proximity and provide navigation and get-off alerts. Boarding always requires your confirmation; Metro Apex does not automatically detect that you boarded.

During active navigation, location may continue to be used while the app is in the background. You can stop the journey or change location permission at any time in iOS Settings.

For nearby-stop and trip-planning requests, the app sends coordinates over an encrypted connection to the Metro Apex service. The service uses them to answer the request and does not intentionally store them in the transit database or application access log. Network infrastructure may transiently process the request to route and protect the service.

Metro Apex also uses Apple MapKit for maps, place search, directions and Apple Maps handoff. If you choose Google Maps handoff, destination information is passed to Google Maps. Apple and Google process information under their own privacy policies.

Network and service information

When the app contacts the Metro Apex service, the service and its providers may process source IP address, request time, method, path, response status, duration, service authorization and rate-limit information. This information is used for service delivery, security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting and reliability, not advertising or tracking.

Application access logs do not intentionally include authorization headers, API keys, request bodies, search terms or location query strings. Beyond Apex does not promise a fixed retention period for operational and security logs. Its retention policy is to keep them only while reasonably needed for service delivery, security, troubleshooting or legal obligations, then delete or de-identify them. Provider-held copies follow the provider’s applicable terms.

Service providers include Cloudflare for network routing and security, the infrastructure provider hosting the Metro Apex service, Apple for iOS, App Store, notification, Live Activity and MapKit services, and Google only when you choose Google Maps handoff. These providers may process information in Australia or other countries where they operate.

Beyond Apex uses these providers only for services needed to operate Metro Apex. Beyond Apex will share user data only with third parties that provide the same or equivalent protection described in this policy and required by applicable App Store rules. Provider-held information is also governed by each provider’s applicable terms, privacy policy and legal obligations.

Feedback and support

Help & Support sends nothing automatically. Feedback text or an issue report leaves your device only when you choose a destination in the iOS share sheet. Technical reports contain a bounded summary of app and OS versions, device family, coarse event age and count, and closed runtime outcome codes. They exclude coordinates, journey history, transit identifiers, searches, credentials, raw errors and stable identifiers. Text you enter may contain information you choose to provide.

Beyond Apex does not promise a fixed retention period for support correspondence. Its retention policy is to keep a message while reasonably needed to respond, resolve the issue, maintain necessary business records or meet legal obligations, then delete or de-identify it.

Disclosure and legal requests

Beyond Apex discloses information only to the providers needed to operate the app, when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a lawful business transfer. Metro Apex does not sell personal information.

Your choices and requests

You can change location or notification permission in iOS Settings, stop live navigation, delete locally saved items, choose whether to share feedback or a journey receipt, and choose whether to open an external maps app.

To ask about access, correction or deletion of personal information, email welcome@beyondapex.com.au. Because Metro Apex has no accounts, Beyond Apex may be unable to identify a network log entry without details such as a relevant IP address and time range, especially after logs have rotated.

Security and children

Metro Apex uses HTTPS transport, short-lived authorization tokens, iOS Keychain storage, access controls and rate limiting. No system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Metro Apex is a general-audience transit utility and is not directed to children. Beyond Apex does not knowingly collect account information from children because the app has no accounts.

Changes

This policy may change when the app, service, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when the policy changes.
